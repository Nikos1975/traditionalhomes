### Selection and processing

`Moni Areti (Crete)00(js).jpg` was selected because it is the strongest legally reusable establishing view and exceeds the 2400 px preference. The source page identifies Jerzy Strzelecki as photographer and the licence as CC BY-SA 3.0.

The 4592 × 2398 px, 6,218,946-byte JPEG was processed with the existing `npm run blog:image` workflow. The resulting responsive WebP files are 480 × 250 (35,840 bytes), 768 × 401 (90,162 bytes), 1200 × 627 (215,454 bytes), 1600 × 836 (380,790 bytes), and 2400 × 1253 (823,050 bytes). The article uses the 1600 px version.

The processing step resized and converted the JPEG without changing its native aspect ratio. The article template presents the image in a centred 16:9 `object-cover` frame, cropping a narrow strip from each side. The visible article credit names the photographer, links the Wikimedia Commons source page, states the CC BY-SA 3.0 licence, discloses the crop, resize and WebP conversion, and applies the same licence to the adapted image.
