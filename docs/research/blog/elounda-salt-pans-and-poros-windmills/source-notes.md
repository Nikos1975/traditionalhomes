### What the licence permits and requires

CC BY-SA 3.0 permits reproduction, redistribution and derivative works,
including local hosting, resizing and format conversion, provided that the
author is credited, the licence is named and linked, and modifications are
indicated. The article caption carries all four elements.

Two author notes on the Commons page were read and are recorded because they
constrain use, even though neither is part of the licence text:

- The author asks that the photograph not be used in social media. This record

  same filename. No upload to Commons was made; only local derivatives were
