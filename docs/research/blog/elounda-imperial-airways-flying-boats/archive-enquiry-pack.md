# Imperial Airways / *Scipio* archival-enquiry package

**Status:** prepared for user review; **no enquiry has been sent.**

**Purpose:** obtain primary records for the 22 August 1936 loss of the Imperial Airways Short S.17 Kent flying boat *Scipio* at Mirabella/Mirabello Bay, Crete, and the associated operational, personnel, timetable, vessel and image record. This is an acquisition package, not evidence that any named institution holds a particular record.

**Research boundary:** the full Inspector of Accidents report has not been located. Hansard remains the accessible official summary. Do not revise the technical accident reconstruction until an actual report is received and reviewed.

> May a research copy be supplied? May it be quoted or reproduced on a commercial hospitality website? We will not assume that Crown copyright, age or online visibility permits reuse.

## 8. Response-intake procedure

For every reply, create a dated intake entry before changing the verification dossier. Preserve the original message or attachment in the approved research location when authorised; do not paraphrase away material details.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Institution | Official institution name |
| Responder | Name and role, only as necessary for the research record |
| Response date | Exact received date |
| Exact wording | Quotation or preserved original-message location |
| Catalogue references | Exact reference, title and covering dates |
| Attachments | Filename, format, page count, SHA-256 if retained |
| Cost | Quoted search/copy/licence cost and currency |
| Access restrictions | Onsite, closure, copyright, use restrictions and delivery terms |
| Copyright/publication terms | Credit, licence, commercial-web permission, resolution and separate-holder conditions |
| Next action | Request, visit, pay, decline or referral |
| Claims affected | Claim-register IDs and whether a status could change |
| Verification-dossier update | Yes/no, with reason and page-level evidence requirement |

Never upgrade a historical claim merely because a catalogue response identifies a file. Update the dossier only after the actual document is obtained and reviewed at page level.

## 9. Sending safeguards

- Do not send an enquiry, submit a form, request a quotation, order a copy or arrange a visit without the user's explicit approval.
- Do not include personal data beyond the sender details required by the chosen institution.
- Do not state that any archive definitely holds the report.
- Do not attach or repeat non-portable `turn...` markers.
- Do not use a supplied research scan in public content until rights and underlying-source requirements are resolved.
