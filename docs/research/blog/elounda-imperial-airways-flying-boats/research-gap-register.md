# Imperial Airways at Elounda: archival research-gap register

**Related evidence record:** [verification dossier](./verification-dossier.md)

**Purpose:** acquire records needed to decide whether the proposed article can safely be drafted. This is a research-control document, not article content.

## 1. Current evidence status

The verification dossier presently supports only the following conclusions:

- Webb Miller travelled through Mirabella in May 1930 on the Imperial Airways India service.
- Miller identifies the pilot as Jimmy Youell; the existing dossier identifies the pilot as A. B. H. Youell, subject to further personnel-record confirmation.
- Miller describes *Imperia* as an old, approximately 100-ton yacht that constituted the air base, and says the airline kept it moored to accommodate pilots and passengers overnight.
- The *Scipio* accident occurred at Mirabella/Mirabello on 22 August 1936.
- Hansard records that the inspector reported a misjudged approach as one reason for the accident and that there was no evidence that any engine failed to respond.

These points do not establish a complete route timetable, direct fuel-transfer method, *Imperia*’s ownership history, detailed accident mechanics, a complete crew list, or a complete rescue account.

Record the repository search URL, search date, result title, reference, access restrictions and requested-copy status. When an item is found, extract:

3. event sequence, official causes and contributory factors;

**Hansard is not a substitute for the full report.** It preserves only a parliamentary question and answer, not the investigation’s full reasoning or appendices.
