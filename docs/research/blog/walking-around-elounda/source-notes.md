# Source Notes

Reviewed 2026-07-23 for the draft article `src/content/blog/walking-around-elounda.md`.

## Method

The preserved HTML is a generated research lead, not evidence. Its useful route names were checked against public institutions, the Municipality of Agios Nikolaos visitor site, the local Agios Nikolaos Mountaineering Club, another recognised Cretan mountaineering club, and general safety guidance from Greek Civil Protection and the US National Park Service.

Organised club reports show that a route was walked by a led group on a particular date. They do not establish present-day access, suitability for an independent walker, or a permanent difficulty rating. Current waymarking and access were therefore treated conservatively.

`claims.json` is the complete material-claim register. `sources.json` records the reviewed sources and their authority. All web references use direct URLs; Google redirect URLs from the raw HTML were not carried into the structured research.

## Safety findings

- Greek Civil Protection advises avoiding long walks or running under the sun during a heatwave and drinking plenty of fluids.
- Greek Civil Protection advises avoiding shore and coastal activities during gale-force winds.
- The Aforesmenos municipal page and mountaineering-club reports independently describe the headland as windy and exposed.
- National Park Service hiking guidance distinguishes ordinary paved walking shoes from sturdy, rubber-soled footwear for dirt and gravel trails.
- The reviewed local sources do not provide a current, official condition bulletin for all four routes.

## Unresolved safety-sensitive points

1. No current official inspection confirms continuous waymarking on the four compared route concepts.
2. No current official source confirms unrestricted access across every track, gate, gorge bed or headland section.
3. Post-storm erosion, vegetation growth and informal diversions have not been independently checked.
4. The raw research's exact times, distances, elevations and difficulty labels were not independently verified and are excluded.
5. The long Vrouchas coastal approach and Kolokytha's unmarked coastal rock section should not be presented as simple walking.
6. Havgas gorge conditions after rain are unresolved; no reader is directed into the gorge during or after severe weather.
