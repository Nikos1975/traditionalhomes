# Topic Brief: Walking routes around Elounda

- Working title: **Walking Around Elounda: Four Routes Between Villages, Hills and Coast**
- Slug: `walking-around-elounda`
- Target article: `src/content/blog/walking-around-elounda.md`
- Intended reader: Visitors who want to compare nearby walking landscapes before deciding whether a village walk, peninsula track, exposed headland or gorge route matches their experience and the day's conditions.
- Article type: Practical area guide and pillar comparison.
- Geographic scope: Mavrikiano and the lower slopes below Oxa; the Kolokytha Peninsula; Vrouchas towards Aforesmenos Lighthouse; and Havgas Gorge between Pines and Plaka.
- Distinct angle: A practical comparison of four different walking landscapes around Elounda. Detailed route instructions remain deferred until access, safety, waymarking and route facts are independently verified.
- Target length: 1,800–2,400 words.
- Publication status: Draft only. No publication or publication pull request.

- Do not present an organised club outing as proof that a route is safe for an independent visitor.
