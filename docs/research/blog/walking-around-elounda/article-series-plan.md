# Elounda walking article series plan

## Pillar article in this pull request

**Walking Around Elounda: Four Routes Between Villages, Hills and Coast**

Purpose: compare four nearby walking landscapes so readers can distinguish village lanes and broad tracks from narrow, rocky, exposed, overgrown or unmarked sections. The article is not a route sheet and does not provide navigation through hazardous sections.

Target path: `src/content/blog/walking-around-elounda.md`

Status: draft only.

## Deferred supporting articles

No supporting article is drafted in this session.

### 1. Mavrikiano and the lower Oxa slopes

Potential question: How can a short village-and-hillside walk stay clearly separate from the more demanding Oxa summit route?

Required before drafting:

- Ground verification of the intended lower-slope line and legal access.
- Current waymarking check.
- Surface, shade and turnaround-point notes.
- Confirmation that the route does not accidentally direct readers onto the summit path.

### 2. Kolokytha Peninsula

Potential question: Which track-and-return option avoids the unmarked coastal rock traverse?

Required before drafting:

- Ground verification of the chosen turnaround.
- Current distinction between signed, visible and unmarked sections.
- Confirmation of access and any seasonal restrictions.
- Explicit exclusion of the exposed coastal traverse unless assessed by a qualified local route authority.

### 3. Vrouchas to Aforesmenos Lighthouse

Potential question: Is there a defensible route for independent walkers, or should this remain a guided outing?

Required before drafting:

- Current access from Vrouchas and from the wind-farm/Agios Ioannis side.
- Legal-access and gate check.
- Waymarking, wind exposure, shade and narrow-path assessment.
- Confirmation of the lighthouse site's safe approach boundary.

### 4. Havgas Gorge between Pines and Plaka

Potential question: What is the current condition of the old route through the gorge?

Required before drafting:

- Post-weather and seasonal condition inspection.
- Vegetation, erosion and gorge-bed assessment.
- Current access and waymarking confirmation.
- Clear wet-weather and post-rain closure rule from a competent local source.

## Linking model

- The pillar will eventually link to each supporting route article only after that article passes its own claim and safety review.
- Supporting articles should link back to the pillar's comparison table.
- Existing contextual links may point to the Mavrikiano guide, Vrouchas guide, and salt-pans/Poros article.
- No future slug is reserved by creating a public or draft article in this session.
