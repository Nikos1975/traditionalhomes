## Detailed request checklists

### A — Cretan State legislation

Ask for original facsimiles or certified catalogue details for Law 375 (isolation/notification), Law 463 (Spinalonga settlement), Regulatory Decree 166, any 1903 internal-operation decree, and accompanying explanatory reports, debates and administrative circulars. For every instrument ask for complete scans including title pages; exact official title; voting and publication dates; Gazette series, issue and page range; amendments; implementing regulations; and copyright/quotation conditions. Record every reported citation as unverified until original pages are obtained and inspected.

### B — First compulsory transfers, 1904

Search with all of these variants, without treating any as fact: October 1904; 13 October; 14 October; December; 251 people; 148 men and 103 women; `meskinies`; Chania; Rethymno; Heraklion; gendarmerie; transport manifest; medical register; admission register; prefectural correspondence; and boat/vessel record. Request nominal transfer lists; departure and arrival dates; sex and age breakdown; origin; transport vessel; escort arrangements; admission register; compulsory-removal correspondence; deaths or exclusions during transfer; and administrative reports after arrival. Do not use 251 as a correct count unless an original record supports it.

### C — Ottoman settlement

Request the 1881 census, the reported 227-family figure, 1,111 versus 1,112 residents, property/house registers, the reported c.200 houses and 25 shops/workshops, occupational registers, customs and port-of-entry records, municipal status/date, Muslim religious and educational buildings, merchants/named residents, and post-1898 emigration or property transfer/expropriation connected with the leprosy settlement. Ask for Ottoman-language originals or translated catalogue references where available. Maintain separate evidence tracks for census, property, commerce and later heritage summaries.

### D — Plaka and Elounda microhistory

Seek named boat owners and boatmen; transport licences; government contracts; food/water suppliers; fishers; guards; postal workers; laundresses; cleaners; builders; medical transport; shopkeepers; priests; administrative staff; burial/cemetery arrangements; mainland disinfection or reception facilities; employment; wartime supply; visiting relatives; and local photographs/private papers. Target municipal minutes, prefectural correspondence, harbour logs, boat licences, contract registers, payment ledgers, Ministry of Health files, newspapers, parish and civil registers, oral-history collections and family archives. The aim is to document named local actors, not repeat a generic statement that boatmen supplied the island.

### E — Treatment and closure

Request medical-register catalogue references; the introduction of sulfone treatment; the terms Promin, diazone and dapsone; start date; selection criteria; discharge/infectiousness assessments; aggregate outcomes; legislation ending compulsory isolation; the formal 1957 closure instrument; Legislative Decree 3766 or any alternative reference; exact closure date; transfer count and names only where lawful; destination institution; remaining priest/caretaker/final resident; post-closure inventory; and record disposal/destruction. Ask explicitly for sensitive-record access restrictions. Do not seek publication of identifiable protected medical data without legal review.

### F — Venetian enclave, 1669–1715

Request original evidence on garrison strength, civilian population, refugees, provisioning, correspondence with Venice, naval movements, churches and quarters, water/food supply, interaction with Ottoman-controlled mainland Crete, siege and surrender correspondence, Zuan Francesco Giustiniani and capitulation terms. Search Italian forms: Spinalonga, fortezza, provveditore, guarnigione, vettovaglie, assedio, resa, Candia and Mirabello. Do not add detailed surrender, captivity or enslavement accounts without an identifiable original record.

### G — Historical images and plans

Request Venetian fortress plans; Ottoman settlement plans/photos; early leprosy-settlement imagery; residents/staff; Plaka boats and landing places; hospital/dormitory imagery; restoration/demolition photographs; and cadastral/architectural drawings. For every possible asset ask for catalogue number, creator, date, rights holder, research-copy fee, high-resolution availability, commercial website permission, licence fee, required credit, crop/edit permission and social-media permission. State that six modern user-owned photographs are already authorised, while every archival asset remains uncleared.
