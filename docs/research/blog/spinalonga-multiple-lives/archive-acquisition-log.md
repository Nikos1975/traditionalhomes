## Sending controls

## Response intake procedure

For every future response, create a dated subheading beneath the relevant tracker ID and retain the original email or letter and every attachment outside the public site tree. Record: sender name and role; date; institution; exact wording or a faithful quoted extract linked to the preserved original; catalogue/fond/series/item references; quoted cost; access restrictions; rights, credit, crop/edit and social-media conditions; and records affected.

Do not replace the original message with a paraphrase. Log whether the response changes the legal-records, numerical-claims, people, contradiction, unresolved-questions, source-bibliography or image-rights register. A response that only identifies a lead remains a lead until the underlying record is inspected.
