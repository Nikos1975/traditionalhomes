# Recommended future article architecture

Working title: **Spinalonga: Why It Was Fortified and How the Island’s Uses Changed**

This is an evidence-led architecture for the provisionally authorised article. The user authorised drafting on 2026-08-06 using verified evidence currently available; unresolved precision must remain qualified, generalised or omitted.

1. **The island at the mouth of Elounda’s harbour** — Open with the rocky islet, sheltered water, Plaka opposite and the central question. Establish that location preceded fortification.
2. **Why Venice fortified Spinalonga** — Place 1579 works in the wider Ottoman-threat context; explain harbour/route control without a single-cause claim.
3. **How the fortress worked** — Read lower enclosure, ridge wall, transverse lines, artillery and cisterns as a response to terrain and naval attack. Use Bressani/Orsini carefully.
4. **A community that remained after the conquest** — Explain the post-1669 Venetian enclave, then make clear which details of supply and civilian life still need research.
5. **Spinalonga as an Ottoman settlement** — Centre homes, shops, port, customs and families; do not treat 1715–1903 as an empty interval.
6. **Why the isolation institution was created there** — Use legal evidence and existing buildings; describe compulsory isolation in period context without euphemism.
7. **Life inside the walls** — Only after new testimony/records are secured. Contrast early deprivation, later self-organisation and institutional changes; use several people.
8. **Plaka, Elounda and the mainland connection** — Boats, supplies, workers and visibility should be documented, not assumed from present visitor routes.
9. **Treatment, change and closure** — Link sulphones, policy change, transfers and 1957 closure with exact records.
10. **From abandonment to archaeological site** — Explain protection, restoration, loss of fabric, management and UNESCO’s tentative—not inscribed—status.
11. **What survives—and what the usual story leaves out** — Return to the same walls serving defence, settlement/trade, confinement/community and heritage/public memory.
