# Editorial policy

This system supports human editorial decisions, never automatic publication. Evidence is ordered: original primary record; official institution; identifiable scholarship; responsible secondary source; attributed testimony; owner observation; research lead; AI synthesis (never public source). Use qualified wording for unresolved precision. Use Hansen’s disease terminology; do not turn illness into entertainment. Confirm image rights; label modern context images and never use them as historical proof. Operational practical details require current verification. Property-sale information is confidential. Do not imitate competitors or copy scripts. A human factual, rights and publication gate is required for every output.
