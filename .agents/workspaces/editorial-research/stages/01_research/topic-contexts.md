Layer 2.5 routing registry for Stage 01. A research project enters the active control plane only by being listed here. Registration is routing, not research: keep topic detail inside the topic contract and its Layer 4 evidence.

## Registration rules

- Register a project only when scoped context materially improves the workflow: a multi-file evidence packet, an open acquisition programme, contested or unresolved claims, controlled image rights, or a published article awaiting evidence-triggered revision.
- Do not register a single-run or small topic. The initiating brief plus the topic folder under `docs/research/` remains the normal Stage 01 path.
- One row per research project. The contract lives at `docs/research/blog/<slug>/CONTEXT.md` beside the evidence it routes to.
- An unregistered folder under `docs/research/` is inert Layer 4 material. It is never loaded because it exists, and it is not part of the active control plane.
- Removing a row deactivates the contract without deleting research. Delete evidence only under a separate approved task.
- A registered contract narrows Stage 01 for one project. It cannot widen stage permissions, authorise drafting, or route to `.agents/workspaces/editorial-research/stages/02_drafting/CONTEXT.md`.
