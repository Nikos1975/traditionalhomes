## Visual Strategy

State the article's visual problem, the minimum useful visual set, what existing assets may already satisfy it, and what must not be depicted. Do not prescribe a number of visuals merely to add variety.

## Rights and Evidence Preconditions

List the claim register, image-rights register, ownership records, archival licences, map-data sources, attribution requirements, and unresolved questions used by this plan.
