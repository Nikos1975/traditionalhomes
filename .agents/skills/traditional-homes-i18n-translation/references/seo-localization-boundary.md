# SEO Localization Boundary

SEO localization is allowed; factual SEO invention is not.

## Allowed

- natural target-language title and meta description;
- target-language H1/headings;
- audience-language generic route segments already supported by the route architecture;
- target-language anchor text;
- terminology chosen for how real users search in that language;
- reordering phrases for natural grammar and intent match.

## Not allowed

- adding claims not supported by the English page;
- changing location/property facts for keyword coverage;
- inventing superlatives, luxury positioning, exclusivity, popularity, or rankings;
- adding a translated URL before a real translated page exists;
- using hreflang to advertise nonexistent pages.

Search adaptation must remain a faithful presentation of the same underlying page.
